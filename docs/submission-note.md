# Submission note (draft, ≤200 words — owner to edit)

**Problem.** Watching a big match apart from your friends loses the best part: teasing the friend whose hero just got out.
Today that means leaving the stream for WhatsApp; by the time you've typed, the moment has gone, and the perfect line rarely comes.

**Hypothesis.** If friends could banter in sync with the ball, without leaving the stream or typing, they would feel like
they watched together, and come back to watch the next match together.

**Solution.** Our Commentary Box is a side panel that sits beside any stream. Each match opens a room. You hold to talk; every clip is
pinned to a ball, so nobody is spoiled on a different delay. AI knows the gang's loyalties, spots the moment that matters to
them (a friend's hero out), and hands you the line. Step away and the gang calls you back for the finish, with a catch-up.
After the match, the room becomes a memory with the night's roast.

**AI used.** Google Gemini writes friends' banter, your tease lines, catch-ups and the wrap, live; Sarvam voices the friends
and transcribes you. Claude (Claude Code) helped design and build it. The gang in the demo is simulated.
