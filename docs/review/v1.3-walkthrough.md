### Behind the scenes, as seen

The log showed every ball: *"Video 13949.0 s → ball 2.4 (read off the scorebug at 13949.0 s). OUT! Rohit lbw to
Topley."* and *"R1 fired: Rohit out for 0 — your hero. Why: a gang member's hero is out."* It also showed ducking
on/off, the voices (*"Dev speaks for 6.9 s · Sarvam bulbul:v4-flash, voice ratan_hi_sports, made in 1.7 s"*), and each
prefetch with its latency and model.

### Finding, fixed during the walkthrough

**The first live AI batch was too big.** It asked for 6 Hinglish lines plus a card at once and timed out at 12.1 s,
so the first minute ran on the **prepared** lines and voices. The safety net did its job, but the first minute should
be live.
- **Fix:** urgent windows now go out as up to 3 lines, with the card in its own parallel request.
- **Re-run on a clean join:** Dev's join line came back **live** (waited 3.1 s), both wicket teases were
  **prefetched 34 s before** the wicket, and the comeback card was **live** (2.9 s). Every Gemini batch took 2.0–3.3 s.

## Reviews

- **/code-review (high):** 10 findings, all fixed; 4 have regression tests in `tests/unit/engine.test.ts`.
  - **Extension join at time 0.** The extension started its room at video time 0, before the first report arrived.
  - **Muted YouTube player.** Leaving the box muted the viewer's own YouTube player.
  - **Language switch race.** An answer in the old language could land after a switch.
  - **Stale missed lines.** Missed lines never cleared after the catch-up.
  - **Seek burst.** A forward seek fired every timed line at once.
  - **Call offer.** The Gang Call offer never expired.
  - **Card lines.** Speaking the comeback reverted live card lines.
  - **Restored audio language.** After a reload, restored audio used the wrong language.
  - **Two cleanups:** director pacing performance, and duplicated helpers.
- **/security-review** (over `170d402..HEAD`): **no findings at confidence ≥ 8.** Nothing Critical or High; nothing to fix.
  11 candidates were considered and dropped with reasons:
  - **Dev-mode CORS** allows `localhost` and any `chrome-extension://` origin, but only when `ALLOWED_ORIGIN` is
    unset, and with no credentials.
  - **Requests without an `Origin` header** pass the origin check. This is unchanged from before, and the check was
    never an auth boundary.
  - **Origin reflection** happens only after an exact allowlist match.
  - **Extension messaging:** there is no `externally_connectable`, reports are validated, and the only command back
    is a clamped volume.
  - **Manifest:** permissions are `sidePanel` only, and the `key` is a public key.
  - **speak/transcribe inputs** are validated against allowlists and enums.
  - **The Hinglish blocklist** can be bypassed, but it is content moderation, not a security boundary.
  - **No keys are bundled** in either build (the bundle check scans both).
  - **API logs** contain no user content.

  The review needed a base ref and this repo has no remote, so a temporary local `origin/HEAD` pointed at `170d402`
  for the run and was deleted afterwards.
